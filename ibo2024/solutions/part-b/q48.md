# Part B — Task 48

*mRNA nucleoside modifications, immune recognition, and antigen translation*

**Official answer: A — True; B — False; C — False; D — False.**

## Reasoning

**A — True.** In Figure 1A, unmodified RNA (UN) activates about 84.5% of the dendritic cells, as judged by simultaneous expression of CD83 and MHC-II above their thresholds. Incorporating modified nucleosides progressively reduces that response: the corresponding activated fractions are about 39.2% for m⁵C, 23.4% for pseudouridine (Ψ), and 5.2% for s²U. Because extracellular RNA can encounter immune cells in body fluids, endogenous RNA that looked strongly immunogenic could provoke inappropriate recognition of the body's own material. The data therefore support the expectation that modifications of cellular mRNA help it avoid such self-directed immune activation.

**B — False.** Low immunogenicity and high antigen production are different requirements. Although s²U produces the smallest dendritic-cell activation in Figure 1A, Figure 1B shows essentially no translation from s²U-containing mRNA. It therefore cannot have the highest efficiency of viral-antigen production. By contrast, Ψ-containing mRNA gives the largest translation signal in the graph, approximately ninefold that of unmodified mRNA.

**C — False.** The comparison favors Ψ over m⁵C on both relevant measures. In Figure 1A, Ψ activates a smaller fraction of dendritic cells than m⁵C (about 23.4% versus 39.2%), while in Figure 1B it yields substantially more translation (roughly ninefold versus fourfold relative to unmodified mRNA). Thus, from the supplied data, an mRNA-vaccine design would be more likely to use pseudouridine than m⁵C, not the reverse.

**D — False.** Leukocytes are nucleated cells with ribosomes and the rest of the translation machinery, so a leukocyte that takes up vaccine mRNA can translate it into antigen. Mature human erythrocytes, however, have lost their ribosomes and most other organelles during differentiation. They therefore cannot translate an introduced mRNA. Since the statement requires both cell types to produce antigen, the inability of erythrocytes makes it false.

**Wording and data qualification.** Figure 1A directly measures activation of dendritic cells, an innate immune response, rather than demonstrating autoimmune disease in an organism; “prevent auto-immune response” in A is therefore the exam's intended broader interpretation. In addition, the figures compare candidate modifications and support a design prediction, but do not directly assay the nucleotide composition of licensed SARS-CoV-2 vaccines. These qualifications do not change the official truth pattern.
