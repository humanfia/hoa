# Part B — Task 2

*Red-deer phylogeny and the interpretation of bootstrap support*

**Official answer: A — True; B — False; C — False; D — False.**

## Reasoning

**A — True.** The value 89 is attached to the branch that groups the *Cervus* samples after the lineage of *Dama dama* splits away. Thus, in 89 of the 100 bootstrap trees, the complete *Cervus* group was recovered with *Dama* as its sister group. In the other 11 resampled trees, that branch was not recovered, so *Dama* was not sister to the complete *Cervus* group in the sense used by the question. Strictly speaking, a bootstrap value measures recovery of the clade defined by a branch; here the rooting and displayed taxon arrangement make that clade-support interpretation equivalent to the stated sister-group relationship.

**B — False.** Closely related subspecies can be distinguished only if the chosen sequence has accumulated enough differences among them. Histone proteins perform highly constrained structural roles in chromatin, so their genes are generally strongly conserved and tend to provide relatively few informative substitutions at this shallow evolutionary scale. Mitochondrial cytochrome-*b* DNA evolves more rapidly and therefore usually supplies more variation for separating populations or subspecies of red deer. Replacing it with histone-gene DNA would not be expected to improve differentiation.

**C — False.** The smallest bootstrap value shown anywhere on the tree is 50, so the branch marked 50 is the most weakly supported displayed branch. Sample 163 occurs in a different, lower subtree and is not a descendant of that branch. It therefore is not part of the tree's least-supported clade.

**D — False.** A bootstrap value of 50 says only that the corresponding branch, and hence its clade, appeared in 50 of the 100 resampled analyses. In the remaining 50 analyses the taxa may have been arranged among several different topologies. The value gives their combined frequency; it neither specifies the alternatives nor implies that exactly one alternative topology exists.
