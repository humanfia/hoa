Let $P\in\R^{d\times d}$ be row-stochastic, so $P_{ij}\geq0$ and $\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}P_{ij}=1$ for each $i$.  In $\C^d\otimes\C^d$, define
$$
    \ket{\psi_i}=\ket{i}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\sqrt{P_{ij}}\ket{j},
    \qquad i=0,
    \dots,d-1.
$$
Prove that $\{\ket{\psi_i}:0\leq i<d\}$ is an orthonormal system.
