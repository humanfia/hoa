Let $U,V\in M_d(\C)$.  The Hilbert--Schmidt inner product on $M_d(\C)$ is
$$
    \langle B,C\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}
    =
    \tr(B^\dagger C).
$$
Prove that transposition preserves this inner product:
$$
    \tr\!\left((U^T)^\dagger V^T\right)
    =
    \tr(U^\dagger V).
$$
Also prove that the transpose map is an isometry for the Hilbert--Schmidt norm:
$$
    \lVert U^T-V^T\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}
    =
    \lVert U-V\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}.
$$
