In single-qubit quantum signal processing, define for $a\in[-1,1]$
$$
    W_x(a)
    =
    \begin{pmatrix}
        a & \ii\sqrt{1-a^2}\\
        \ii\sqrt{1-a^2} & a
    \end{pmatrix}.
$$
For a phase sequence
$$
    \vec\phi=(\phi_0,\phi_1,\dots,\phi_k)\in\R^{k+1},
$$
let
$$
    Z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
$$
and define
$$
    U_{\vec\phi}(a)
    =
    e^{\ii\phi_0 Z}
    \prod_{j=1}^k
    W_x(a)e^{\ii\phi_jZ},
    \qquad
    A(a)=\bra{0}U_{\vec\phi}(a)\ket{0}.
$$
Prove that $A(a)$ is a complex polynomial in $a$ of degree at most $k$, and that its parity agrees with the parity of $k$:
$$
    A(-a)=(-1)^kA(a).
$$

Independently, let
$$
    P(a)
    =
    \sum_{\ell=-k}^{k}
    \frac{k-|\ell|+1}{(k+1)^2}
    e^{2\ii\ell\arccos(a)}.
$$
Set $\theta=2\arccos(a)$.  Prove that
$$
    P(a)
    =
    \frac1{(k+1)^2}
    \left|
        \sum_{m=0}^{k}e^{\ii m\theta}
    \right|^2.
$$
Prove that $0\leq P(a)\leq1$ for all $a\in[-1,1]$, and that $P(1)=1$.
