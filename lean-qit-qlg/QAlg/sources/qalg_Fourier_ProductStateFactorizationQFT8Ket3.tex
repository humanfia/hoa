Define
$$
    \QFT_8\ket{x}=\frac1{\sqrt8}\sum_{y=0}^7 e^{2\pi\ii xy/8}\ket{y}.
$$
Work with the big-endian convention $\ket y=\ket{y_2y_1y_0}$, where $y=4y_2+2y_1+y_0$.  Prove the corrected product factorization
$$
    \QFT_8\ket3=
    \frac{\ket0+e^{2\pi\ii(1/2)}\ket1}{\sqrt2}
    \otimes
    \frac{\ket0+e^{2\pi\ii(3/4)}\ket1}{\sqrt2}
    \otimes
    \frac{\ket0+e^{2\pi\ii(3/8)}\ket1}{\sqrt2},
$$
with tensor factors ordered as qubits $2,1,0$.
