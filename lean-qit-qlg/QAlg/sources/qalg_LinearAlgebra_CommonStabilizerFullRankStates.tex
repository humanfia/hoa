Let $\mathcal H$ be a finite $d$-dimensional Hilbert space.  Let $\rho$ and $\sigma$ be full-rank density operators on $\mathcal H$, both with simple spectra:
$$
    \rho=\sum_{i=1}^d r_i\ketbra{r_i}{r_i},
    \qquad
    \sigma=\sum_{j=1}^d s_j\ketbra{s_j}{s_j},
$$
where $r_i\neq r_{i'}$ for $i\neq i'$ and $s_j\neq s_{j'}$ for $j\neq j'$.  Assume the two eigenbases have no zero cross-overlaps:
$$
    \braket{r_i}{s_j}\neq0
    \qquad
    \text{for all }i,j\in\{1,\dots,d\}.
$$
Let $W$ be a unitary satisfying
$$
    W\rho W^\dagger=\rho,
    \qquad
    W\sigma W^\dagger=\sigma.
$$
Prove that $W$ must be a scalar unitary:
$$
    W=e^{\ii\theta}I
$$
for some $\theta\in\R$.  Equivalently, prove that the common unitary stabilizer of $\rho$ and $\sigma$ is exactly $U(1)I$.
