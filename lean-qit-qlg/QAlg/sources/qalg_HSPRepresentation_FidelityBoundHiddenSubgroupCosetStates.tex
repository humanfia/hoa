Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $H,H'\leq G$.  For a subgroup $K\leq G$, define
$$
    \ket{gK}
    =
    \frac1{\sqrt{|K|}}\sum_{k\in K}\ket{gk},
    \qquad
    \rho_K=
    \frac1{|G|}
    \sum_{g\in G}\ketbra{gK}{gK}.
$$
Let $\Pi_{\rho_H}$ denote the orthogonal projector onto the support of $\rho_H$.  For density operators $\rho,\sigma$ on the same finite-dimensional Hilbert space, define the fidelity convention
$$
    F(\rho,\sigma)=\tr\!\sqrt{\sqrt{\rho}\,\sigma\sqrt{\rho}} .
$$
Assume the fidelity upper bound
$$
    F(\rho_H,\rho_{H'})^2
    \leq
    \tr(\Pi_{\rho_H}\rho_{H'})
$$
and the coset identities
$$
    \braket{gH}{g'H'}
    =
    \frac{|gH\cap g'H'|}{\sqrt{|H||H'|}},
    \qquad
    |HH'|=\frac{|H||H'|}{|H\cap H'|}.
$$
Prove that
$$
    F(\rho_H,\rho_{H'})
    \leq
    \sqrt{\frac{|H\cap H'|}{|H|}}.
$$
