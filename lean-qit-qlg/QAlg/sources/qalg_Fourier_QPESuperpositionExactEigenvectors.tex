Let $U$ have orthonormal eigenvectors $\ket{u_1},\ket{u_2}$ with eigenvalues $e^{2\pi\ii\phi_1}$ and $e^{2\pi\ii\phi_2}$.  Suppose $N=2^n$ and $N\phi_1,N\phi_2\in\Z$.  Phase estimation is run with $n$ counting qubits and target state
$$
    \ket\psi=\alpha\ket{u_1}+\beta\ket{u_2},
    \qquad |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1.
$$
Prove that immediately before measurement the joint state is
$$
    \alpha\ket{N\phi_1}\ket{u_1}+\beta\ket{N\phi_2}\ket{u_2},
$$
where $\ket{N\phi_j}$ denotes the computational basis state of the counting register labeled by the integer $N\phi_j$.
