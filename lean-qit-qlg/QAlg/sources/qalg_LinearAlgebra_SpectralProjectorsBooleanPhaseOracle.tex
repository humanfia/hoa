Let $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ be a Boolean function and define the phase oracle
$$
    O_f\ket{x}=(-1)^{f(x)}\ket{x},\qquad x\in\{0,1\}^n .
$$
Prove that the orthogonal projectors onto the $+1$- and $-1$-eigenspaces of $O_f$ are
$$
    \Pi_+=\frac{\id+O_f}{2},\qquad
    \Pi_- =\frac{\id-O_f}{2}.
$$
Also prove the exact trace formula
$$
    \tr(O_f)=2^n-2\,|f^{-1}(1)|.
$$
