Let $U$ be an $n$-qubit unitary with spectral decomposition
$$
    U=\sum_j e^{\ii x_j}\ketbra{\psi_j}{\psi_j}.
$$
Define the controlled unitary
$$
    C(U)=\ketbra{0}{0}\otimes I+\ketbra{1}{1}\otimes U.
$$
Prove that each two-dimensional subspace
$$
    \operatorname{span}\{\ket{0}\ket{\psi_j},\ket{1}\ket{\psi_j}\}
$$
is invariant under $C(U)$, and that the matrix of $C(U)$ on this subspace is
$$
    \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&e^{\ii x_j}\end{pmatrix}.
$$
