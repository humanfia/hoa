Let $\rho$ be a density matrix and $O$ an observable on $\C^2\otimes\C^2\otimes\mathcal K$, where $\mathcal K$ is an arbitrary finite-dimensional spectator space.  Define
$$
    \begin{aligned}
    X&=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
    &
    Z&=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\\
    V(\theta)&=\bigl(R_X(\theta)\otimes R_Z(\theta)\bigr)\otimes I_{\mathcal K},
    &
    \mathcal L(\theta)&=\tr\!\left[O\,V(\theta)\rho V(\theta)^\dagger\right],
    \end{aligned}
$$
where $R_P(\theta)=e^{-\ii P\theta/2}$ for $P\in\{X,Z\}$.  Prove the parameter-shift identity
$$
    \frac{d}{d\theta}\mathcal L(\theta)
    =
    \frac{2+\sqrt2}{4}
    \left[
        \mathcal L\!\left(\theta+\frac\pi4\right)
        -
        \mathcal L\!\left(\theta-\frac\pi4\right)
    \right]
    +
    \frac{2-\sqrt2}{4}
    \left[
        \mathcal L\!\left(\theta-\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)
        -
        \mathcal L\!\left(\theta+\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)
    \right].
$$
