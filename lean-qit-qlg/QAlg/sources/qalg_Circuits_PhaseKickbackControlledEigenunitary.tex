Let $\mathcal H_T$ be a finite-dimensional target Hilbert space and let $\mathcal H_C\cong\C^2$ be a single-qubit control Hilbert space.  Let $U$ be a unitary on $\mathcal H_T$, and suppose $\ket{\psi}\in\mathcal H_T$ is a normalized eigenvector:
$$
    U\ket{\psi}=e^{\ii\theta}\ket{\psi},
    \qquad
    \theta\in[0,2\pi).
$$
Define
$$
    CU=\ketbra{0}{0}_C\otimes I_T+\ketbra{1}{1}_C\otimes U.
$$
Let the control qubit be initialized as
$$
    \ket{\phi}_C=\alpha\ket0_C+\beta\ket1_C,
    \qquad
    |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1.
$$
Prove the phase-kickback identity
$$
    CU(\ket{\phi}_C\otimes\ket{\psi}_T)
    =
    \left(\alpha\ket0_C+\beta e^{\ii\theta}\ket1_C\right)
    \otimes\ket{\psi}_T.
$$
In particular, prove that the output remains separable and that the eigenvalue phase is transferred to the relative phase of the control register.
