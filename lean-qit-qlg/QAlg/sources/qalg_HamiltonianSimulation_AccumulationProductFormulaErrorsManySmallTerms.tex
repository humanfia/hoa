Let $H_1,\dots,H_M$ be Hermitian matrices with $\opnorm{H_j}\leq\gamma$, where $0<\gamma<1$.  Put
$$
    S_k=\sum_{j=1}^k H_j,
    \qquad S_0=0.
$$
Fix $R>0$ and assume $M\gamma\leq R$.  Assume that for each $k=1,\dots,M$ there is a remainder $F_k$ satisfying
$$
    e^{S_{k-1}}e^{H_k}=e^{S_k}+F_k,
    \qquad
    \opnorm{F_k}\leq C\opnorm{S_{k-1}}\opnorm{H_k}
        e^{\opnorm{S_{k-1}}+\opnorm{H_k}} .
$$
For the error $E_M$ defined by
$$
    e^{H_1}e^{H_2}\cdots e^{H_M}=e^{S_M}+E_M,
$$
prove the explicit bound
$$
    \opnorm{E_M}
    \leq
    \frac{C e^R}{2}M(M-1)\gamma^2 .
$$
In particular, when $R$ is fixed, $\opnorm{E_M}=O_R(M^2\gamma^2)$.
