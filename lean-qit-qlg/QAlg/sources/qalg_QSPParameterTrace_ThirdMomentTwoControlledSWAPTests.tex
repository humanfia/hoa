Let $\rho$ be a density matrix on $\mathcal H$, and let $B_1,B_2,B_3$ be three registers isomorphic to $\mathcal H$.  Let $A_1,A_2$ be two auxiliary qubits initialized in $\ket{+}\ket{+}$, and define
$$
    \sigma_{\mathrm{in}}
    =
    \ketbra{+}{+}_{A_1}\otimes\ketbra{+}{+}_{A_2}
    \otimes \rho_{B_1}\otimes\rho_{B_2}\otimes\rho_{B_3}.
$$
Let
$$
    U_1=\ketbra{0}{0}_{A_1}\otimes I+\ketbra{1}{1}_{A_1}\otimes S_{1,2},
    \qquad
    U_2=\ketbra{0}{0}_{A_2}\otimes I+\ketbra{1}{1}_{A_2}\otimes S_{1,3},
$$
where $S_{i,j}$ swaps $B_i$ and $B_j$.  Set
$$
    \sigma_{\mathrm{out}}=U_2U_1\sigma_{\mathrm{in}}U_1^\dagger U_2^\dagger .
$$
Let $X=\ketbra{0}{1}+\ketbra{1}{0}$.  Prove that
$$
    \tr\!\left[
        \bigl(X_{A_1}\otimes X_{A_2}\otimes I_{B_1B_2B_3}\bigr)
        \sigma_{\mathrm{out}}
    \right]
    =
    \tr(\rho^3).
$$
