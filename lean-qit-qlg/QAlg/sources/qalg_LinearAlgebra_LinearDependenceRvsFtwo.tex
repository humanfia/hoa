Let
$$
    v_1=(1,1,0)^T,
    \qquad
    v_2=(0,1,1)^T,
    \qquad
    v_3=(1,0,1)^T.
$$
Regard these vectors either as elements of $\R^3$ or as elements of $\F_2^3$.  Prove that $\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$ is linearly independent over $\R$ but linearly dependent over $\F_2$.  Also determine the full set $\operatorname{span}_{\F_2}\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$.
