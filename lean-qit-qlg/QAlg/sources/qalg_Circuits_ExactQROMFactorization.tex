Let $\mathcal H_A=(\C^2)^{\otimes n}$ be an address register and $\mathcal H_D=(\C^2)^{\otimes m}$ a data register.  Let
$$
    D:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}^m
$$
be a classical data function.  The ideal QROM unitary is
$$
    U_D(\ket{x}_A\otimes\ket{y}_D)
    =
    \ket{x}_A\otimes\ket{y\oplus D(x)}_D.
$$
Identify each address integer $k\in\{0,1,\dots,2^n-1\}$ with its $n$-bit computational basis string, and write $D(k)=D_1(k)\cdots D_m(k)$.  Let
$$
    X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
For each address $k$, define
$$
    C_k
    =
    I_A\otimes I_D
    +
    \ketbra{k}{k}_A
    \otimes
    \left(X^{D(k)}-I_D\right),
    \qquad
    X^{D(k)}=\bigotimes_{j=1}^m X^{D_j(k)}.
$$
Prove that each $C_k$ is unitary and acts on computational basis states as
$$
    C_k(\ket{x}_A\otimes\ket{y}_D)
    =
    \begin{cases}
    \ket{k}_A\otimes\ket{y\oplus D(k)}_D, & x=k,\\[1mm]
    \ket{x}_A\otimes\ket{y}_D, & x\neq k.
    \end{cases}
$$
Prove the exact factorization
$$
    U_D=\prod_{k=0}^{2^n-1}C_k,
$$
and prove that $C_kC_\ell=C_\ell C_k$ for all $k,\ell$, so the product ordering has no effect.
