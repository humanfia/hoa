Let $G$ be a finite group, $H\leq G$, and let $\sigma:G\to\mathcal U(V_\sigma)$ be an irreducible unitary representation of dimension $d_\sigma$.  Define
$$
    \sigma(H)=\sum_{h\in H}\sigma(h).
$$
Assume
$$
    \sum_{h\in H}\chi_\sigma(h)^*\neq0,
$$
and define the conditional strong-Fourier state
$$
    \widehat\rho_{H,\sigma}
    =
    \frac{\sigma(H)^*}
    {\sum_{h\in H}\chi_\sigma(h)^*}.
$$
Prove that
$$
    P_{H,\sigma}=\frac1{|H|}\sigma(H)
$$
is the orthogonal projector onto the $H$-invariant subspace
$$
    V_\sigma^H=\{v\in V_\sigma:\sigma(h)v=v\text{ for all }h\in H\}.
$$
Prove that
$$
    \widehat\rho_{H,\sigma}
    =
    \frac{P_{H,\sigma}^*}{\rank(P_{H,\sigma})}
$$
and prove the rank formula
$$
    \rank(\widehat\rho_{H,\sigma})
    =
    \rank(P_{H,\sigma})
    =
    \frac1{|H|}\sum_{h\in H}\chi_\sigma(h).
$$
Equivalently, under the conjugated convention in the definition of $\widehat\rho_{H,\sigma}$, the same rank is
$$
    \frac1{|H|}\sum_{h\in H}\chi_\sigma(h)^*.
$$
