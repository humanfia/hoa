Let $U$ be a unitary with spectral decomposition
$$
    U=\sum_j e^{\ii x_j}\ketbra{\psi_j}{\psi_j},
    \qquad x_j\in[-\pi,\pi).
$$
Let
$$
    P(x)=\sum_{m=-L}^{L}c_m e^{\ii mx}
$$
be a trigonometric polynomial with $|P(x)|\leq1$, and use
$$
    Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\ii\\ \ii&0\end{pmatrix},
    \qquad
    Z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
    \qquad
    R_y(\alpha)=e^{-\ii\alpha Y/2},
    \qquad
    R_z(\alpha)=e^{-\ii\alpha Z/2}.
$$
Suppose a one-qubit trigonometric-QSP sequence with gates $A_\ell=R_y(\theta_\ell)R_z(\phi_\ell)$ has amplitude
$$
    \bra0 W(x)\ket0=P(x),
$$
where $W(x)$ is obtained by applying, in order, the gates
$$
    A_{2L},\,R_z(x),\,A_{2L-1},\,R_z(x),\,\ldots,\,
    A_2,\,R_z(x),\,A_1,\,R_z(x),\,A_0 .
$$
Number the $2L$ occurrences of $R_z(x)$ from left to right.  Define the controlled spectral circuit $\mathcal W_U$ by replacing each odd-numbered occurrence of $R_z(x)$ by
$$
    C_1(U)=\ketbra{0}{0}\otimes I+\ketbra{1}{1}\otimes U
$$
and each even-numbered occurrence by
$$
    C_0(U^\dagger)=\ketbra{0}{0}\otimes U^\dagger+\ketbra{1}{1}\otimes I,
$$
with the same one-qubit rotations $A_\ell$ on the control qubit.  Prove that
$$
    (\bra0\otimes I)\mathcal W_U(\ket0\otimes I)=P(U),
    \qquad
    P(U)=\sum_{m=-L}^{L}c_m U^m .
$$
Here $U^m=(U^\dagger)^{-m}$ for $m<0$.
Consequently, for every input state $\ket\psi$ with $P(U)\ket\psi\neq0$, postselecting the control qubit on outcome $0$ prepares
$$
    \frac{P(U)\ket\psi}{\norm{P(U)\ket\psi}_2} .
$$
