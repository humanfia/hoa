Let $\mathcal H=\C^d$ with computational basis $\{\ket j\}_{j=0}^{d-1}$, where $d\geq2$, and let
$$
    \omega=e^{2\pi\ii/d}.
$$
Define the clock and shift operators
$$
    Z
    =
    \sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\omega^j\ketbra{j}{j},
    \qquad
    X
    =
    \sum_{j=0}^{d-1}
    \ketbra{j+1\bmod d}{j}.
$$
For $U\in M_d(\C)$, define
$$
    \mathcal T(U)
    =
    \frac1d
    \sum_{j,k=0}^{d-1}
    Z^jX^kUZ^{-j}X^{-k}.
$$
Prove $ZX=\omega XZ$.  Compute the above average explicitly for $U=X$ and prove that
$$
    \mathcal T(X)=X.
$$
Also prove that
$$
    X^T=X^{-1}=X^{d-1}.
$$
Prove that the identity $\mathcal T(U)=U^T$ cannot hold for all $U$ when $d>2$.
