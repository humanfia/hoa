Let
$$
    \begin{aligned}
    X&=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
    &
    Y&=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\ii\\ \ii&0\end{pmatrix},\\
    R_y(\theta)&=\exp\!\left(-\ii\frac{\theta}{2}Y\right),
    &
    \operatorname{CNOT}_{C\to T}
    &=
    \ketbra{0}{0}_C\otimes I_T
    +
    \ketbra{1}{1}_C\otimes X_T .
    \end{aligned}
$$
Define
$$
    C(R_y(\theta))
    =
    \ketbra{0}{0}_C\otimes I_T
    +
    \ketbra{1}{1}_C\otimes R_y(\theta)_T .
$$
Operator products in the displayed circuit are composed right-to-left.  Prove the exact identity
$$
    C(R_y(\theta))
    =
    \left(I_C\otimes R_y\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)_T\right)
    \operatorname{CNOT}_{C\to T}
    \left(I_C\otimes R_y\!\left(-\frac{\theta}{2}\right)_T\right)
    \operatorname{CNOT}_{C\to T}.
$$
