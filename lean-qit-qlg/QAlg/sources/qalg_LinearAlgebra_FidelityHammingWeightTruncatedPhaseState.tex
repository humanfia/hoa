For fixed $x\in\{0,1\}^n$, define
$$
    \ket{\psi_x}=2^{-n/2}\sum_{y\in\{0,1\}^n}(-1)^{x\cdot y}\ket{y},
$$
where $x\cdot y$ is the inner product over $\F_2^n$.  For $0\leq r\leq n$, define
$$
    \ket{\psi_x^{(r)}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{R_{n,r}}}
    \sum_{\substack{y\in\{0,1\}^n\\ |y|\leq r}}(-1)^{x\cdot y}\ket{y},
    \qquad
    R_{n,r}=\sum_{i=0}^r\binom{n}{i}.
$$
Prove that
$$
    \left|\braket{\psi_x}{\psi_x^{(r)}}\right|^2=\frac{R_{n,r}}{2^n},
$$
independently of $x$.  Prove also that for every $\eta\in(0,1)$ there exists a constant $c_\eta>0$ such that, for
$$
    r_n=\left\lfloor \frac n2+c_\eta\sqrt n\right\rfloor,
$$
one has $\liminf_{n\to\infty}R_{n,r_n}/2^n\geq 1-\eta$.
