Let $L$ be a finite index set, let $\alpha_\ell>0$, and let $U_\ell$ be unitary operators on a system Hilbert space $\mathcal H_S$.  Define
$$
    A=\sum_{\ell\in L}\alpha_\ell U_\ell,
    \qquad
    \alpha=\sum_{\ell\in L}\alpha_\ell.
$$
Let an ancilla Hilbert space have computational basis states $\ket\ell$ for $\ell\in L$, and suppose a unitary $P$ satisfies
$$
    P\ket0=\frac1{\sqrt\alpha}\sum_{\ell\in L}\sqrt{\alpha_\ell}\ket\ell.
$$
Define
$$
    \operatorname{SELECT}
    =
    \sum_{\ell\in L}\ketbra{\ell}{\ell}\otimes U_\ell
$$
and
$$
    W=(P^\dagger\otimes I_S)\operatorname{SELECT}(P\otimes I_S).
$$
Prove that $W$ is a perfect $(\alpha,a,0)$-block encoding of $A$, where $a$ is the number of ancilla qubits, by showing
$$
    (\bra0\otimes I_S)W(\ket0\otimes I_S)=\frac{A}{\alpha}.
$$
