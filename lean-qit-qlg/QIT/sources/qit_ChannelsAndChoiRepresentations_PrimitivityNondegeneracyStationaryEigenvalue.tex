Let $\cE:\cL(\cH)\to\cL(\cH)$ be a completely positive trace-preserving map. Say that $\cE$ is primitive if there exists an integer $n>0$ such that
$$
\cE^n(\rho)>0
\qquad
\text{for every }\rho\in\cS(\cH),
$$
where $X>0$ means that $X$ is positive definite. Prove that if $\cE$ is primitive, then the eigenvalue $1$ of the superoperator $\cE$ is nondegenerate.
