A quantum channel $\cE:\cL(\cH)\to \cL(\cH)$ is called unital if
$$
\cE(\Id)=\Id.
$$
Let $\dim(\cH)=d$. Prove that there exists a finite set of unitary operators
$$
\{U_i\}_{i=1}^{d^2}
$$
and a uniform probability distribution
$$
p_i=\frac{1}{d^2}
$$
such that, for every $A\in \cL(\cH)$,
$$
\frac{1}{d^2}\sum_{i=1}^{d^2} U_i A U_i^\dagger
=
\Tr(A)\frac{\Id}{d}.
$$
In other words, prove that the completely depolarizing channel can be written as a random unitary channel.
