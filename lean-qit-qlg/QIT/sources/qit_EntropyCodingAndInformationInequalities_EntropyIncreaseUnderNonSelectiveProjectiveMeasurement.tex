Let $P$ be an orthogonal projector on $\cH$, and let
$$
Q=\Id-P.
$$
Define the non-selective projective measurement channel
$$
\cM(\rho)=P\rho P+Q\rho Q.
$$

Using the random unitary representation from Problem~\ref{prob:qit-entropy-04} and the concavity of the von Neumann entropy, prove that
$$
S(\cM(\rho))\geq S(\rho).
$$
That is, prove that a non-selective projective measurement cannot decrease the entropy of a quantum state.
