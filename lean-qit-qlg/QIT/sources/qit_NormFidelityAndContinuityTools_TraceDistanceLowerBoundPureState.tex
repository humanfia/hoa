Let
$$
\rho=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}
$$
be a pure state, and let $\sigma\in \cS(\cH)$ be an arbitrary density operator. The trace distance is defined by
$$
D(\rho,\sigma)
=
\frac{1}{2}\|\rho-\sigma\|_1.
$$
The fidelity between a pure state and $\sigma$ is defined by
$$
F(\ket{\psi},\sigma)
=
\sqrt{\bra{\psi}\sigma\ket{\psi}}.
$$

Prove the stronger lower bound
$$
1-F(\ket{\psi},\sigma)^2
\leq
D(\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi},\sigma).
$$
