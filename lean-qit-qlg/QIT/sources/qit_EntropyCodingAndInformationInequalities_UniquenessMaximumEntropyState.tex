Let $\rho\in \cS(\cH)$ be a density operator on a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space, and suppose that
$$
\rho\neq \frac{\Id}{d}.
$$
Using the random unitary identity
$$
\frac{1}{d^2}\sum_{i=1}^{d^2} U_i \rho U_i^\dagger
=
\frac{\Id}{d},
$$
together with the strict concavity of the von Neumann entropy, prove that
$$
S(\rho)<\log_2 d.
$$
Conclude that the completely mixed state $\Id/d$ is the unique state of maximum entropy.
