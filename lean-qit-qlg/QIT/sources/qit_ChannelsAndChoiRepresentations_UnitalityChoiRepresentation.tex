Let $\cE:\cL(\cH_A)\to\cL(\cH_B)$ be a completely positive map with
$\dim\cH_A=\dim\cH_B=d$. Fix an orthonormal computational basis
$\{\ket{i}\}_{i=1}^d$ for both copies, let
$$
\ket{\Gamma}_{AB}=\sum_{i=1}^{d}\ket{i}_A\ket{i}_B,
$$
and define the unnormalized Choi matrix
$$
J_{\cE}=(\id_A\otimes\cE)(\ket{\Gamma}\bra{\Gamma}_{AB}).
$$
Prove that $\cE$ is unital,
$$
\cE(\Id)=\Id,
$$
if and only if
$$
\Tr_A(J_{\cE})=\Id_B.
$$
