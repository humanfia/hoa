Consider an i.i.d. single-qubit quantum source with average density matrix
$$
\rho
=
\frac{1}{4}
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & 1\\
1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Prove that the eigenvalues of $\rho$ are
$$
\lambda_{\pm}
=
\frac{2\pm \sqrt{2}}{4}.
$$
Then compute the von Neumann entropy
$$
S(\rho)
=
-\lambda_+\log_2\lambda_+
-\lambda_-\log_2\lambda_-.
$$
