Let $\cH_R$ and $\cH_Q$ be isomorphic Hilbert spaces of dimension $d$. Let
$$
\{\ket{i_R}\}_{i=1}^{d}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\{\ket{i_Q}\}_{i=1}^{d}
$$
be fixed computational bases for $R$ and $Q$, respectively. Define the unnormalized maximally entangled state
$$
\ket{m}
=
\sum_{i=1}^{d}
\ket{i_R}\otimes \ket{i_Q}
\in \cH_R\otimes \cH_Q.
$$

Let $A\in \cL(\cH_R)$ and $B\in \cL(\cH_Q)$. After identifying $\cH_R\cong \cH_Q$ through the chosen bases, prove the identity
$$
\bra{m}(A\otimes B)\ket{m}
=
\Tr(A^T B).
$$
Equivalently, prove that
$$
\bra{m}(A^\ast\otimes B)\ket{m}
=
\Tr(A^\dagger B),
$$
where $A^T$ denotes the transpose of $A$, and $A^\ast$ denotes the entrywise complex conjugate of $A$, both with respect to the chosen basis.
