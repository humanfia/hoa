Let $\cH_A$ and $\cH_B$ be finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. The partial trace over $B$ is the linear map
$$
\Tr_B:
\cL(\cH_A\otimes\cH_B)
\to
\cL(\cH_A).
$$
Prove that $\Tr_B$ is completely positive and trace-preserving by giving an explicit Kraus representation after fixing an orthonormal basis of $\cH_B$.
