Let
$$
\Phi:\cL(\cH_A)\to\cL(\cH_B)
$$
be a linear map, with $d_A=\dim\cH_A$. Fix an orthonormal computational basis
$\{\ket{i}\}_{i=1}^{d_A}$ for $\cH_A$, let $A'\cong A$, and define
$$
\ket{\Gamma}_{AA'}=\sum_{i=1}^{d_A}\ket{i}_A\ket{i}_{A'}.
$$
The unnormalized Choi matrix of $\Phi$ is
$$
J_{\Phi}
=
(\id_A\otimes\Phi_{A'\to B})
(\ket{\Gamma}\bra{\Gamma}_{AA'}).
$$
The map $\Phi$ is completely positive if
$$
\id_R\otimes\Phi
$$
is positive on $\cL(\cH_R\otimes\cH_A)$ for every finite-dimensional Hilbert space $\cH_R$.

Prove that $\Phi$ is completely positive if and only if
$$
J_{\Phi}\in\Pos(\cH_A\otimes\cH_B).
$$
