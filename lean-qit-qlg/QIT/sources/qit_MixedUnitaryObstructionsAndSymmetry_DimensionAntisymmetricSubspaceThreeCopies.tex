Let $\cH$ be a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space. The antisymmetric subspace of $\cH^{\otimes3}$ is
$$
\ASym^3(\cH)
=
\left\{
\ket{\psi}\in\cH^{\otimes3}:
W^\pi\ket{\psi}=\sgn(\pi)\ket{\psi}\text{ for all }\pi\in S_3
\right\}.
$$
Prove that
$$
\dim\ASym^3(\cH)
=
\binom{d}{3}
=
\frac{d(d-1)(d-2)}{6}.
$$
