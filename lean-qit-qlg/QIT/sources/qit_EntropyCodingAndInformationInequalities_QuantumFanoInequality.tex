Let $\rho_A\in \cS(\cH_A)$ be a density operator on a $d$-dimensional system $A$. Let
$$
\ket{\psi}_{RA}\in \cH_R\otimes \cH_A
$$
be a purification of $\rho_A$, where $\cH_R\cong \cH_A$. Let
$$
\cE_{A\to B}:\cL(\cH_A)\to \cL(\cH_B)
$$
be a quantum channel with $\cH_B\cong \cH_A$. Define
$$
\rho_{RB}
=
(\id_R\otimes \cE_{A\to B})
\left(
\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}_{RA}
\right).
$$
The exchange entropy is
$$
S(R,B)=S(\rho_{RB}),
$$
and the entanglement fidelity is
$$
F_e
=
\bra{\psi}\rho_{RB}\ket{\psi}.
$$

Let
$$
P=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi},
\qquad
Q=\Id-P.
$$
By considering the binary partition $\{P,Q\}$ on the joint system $RB$, prove the Quantum Fano Inequality
$$
S(\rho_{RB})
\leq
H(F_e)
+
(1-F_e)\log_2(d^2-1),
$$
where
$$
H(F_e)
=
-F_e\log_2 F_e
-
(1-F_e)\log_2(1-F_e)
$$
is the binary entropy.
