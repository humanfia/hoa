Let $\cE:\cL(\cH)\to\cL(\cH)$ be a completely positive map on a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space. Let
$$
\ket{\Gamma}=\sum_{i=1}^{d}\ket{i}\otimes\ket{i}
$$
and define the Choi matrix
$$
J_{\cE}=(\idmap\otimes\cE)\bigl(\ket{\Gamma}\bra{\Gamma}\bigr).
$$
Prove that $\cE$ is trace-preserving if and only if
$$
\Tr_B(J_{\cE})=\Id_A.
$$
