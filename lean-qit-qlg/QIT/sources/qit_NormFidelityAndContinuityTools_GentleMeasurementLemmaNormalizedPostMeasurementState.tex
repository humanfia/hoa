Let $\rho\in \cS(\cH)$ be a density operator, and let $M$ be a positive operator satisfying
$$
0\leq M\leq \Id.
$$
Suppose that the measurement outcome corresponding to $M$ occurs with high probability:
$$
\Tr(M\rho)\geq 1-\epsilon,
\qquad
\epsilon\in [0,1].
$$

Using the canonical square-root measurement instrument, define the normalized post-measurement state
$$
\rho'
=
\frac{\sqrt{M}\rho\sqrt{M}}{\Tr(M\rho)}.
$$

Prove that
$$
\|\rho'-\rho\|_1
\leq
2\sqrt{\epsilon}+\epsilon.
$$
