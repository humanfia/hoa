Let $P$ be an orthogonal projector on $\cH$, and let
$$
Q=\Id-P.
$$
A non-selective projective measurement maps a density operator $\rho$ to
$$
\cM(\rho)
=
P\rho P+Q\rho Q.
$$

Prove that there exist two unitary operators $U_1,U_2$ and a probability $p\in[0,1]$ such that, for every density operator $\rho$,
$$
P\rho P+Q\rho Q
=
p\,U_1\rho U_1^\dagger
+
(1-p)\,U_2\rho U_2^\dagger.
$$
