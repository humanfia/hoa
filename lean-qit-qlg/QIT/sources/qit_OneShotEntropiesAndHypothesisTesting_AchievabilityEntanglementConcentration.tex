\Section{Achievability of Entanglement Concentration}
Let
$$
\lvert \psi\rangle_{AB}=\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}\sqrt{p(x)}\lvert x\rangle_A\lvert x\rangle_B
$$
be a bipartite pure state with Schmidt coefficients $\{p(x)\}_{x\in\mathcal{X}}$.  Its entanglement entropy is
$$
E(\psi)=S(\psi_A)=-\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}p(x)\log_2p(x).
$$
Let
$$
\lvert \Phi_M\rangle_{AB}=\frac{1}{\sqrt M}\sum_{j=1}^{M}\lvert j\rangle_A\lvert j\rangle_B
$$
be the standard maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank $M$.
Prove that for every rate
$$
0\le R<E(\psi),
$$
there exists a sequence of LOCC channels $\{\Lambda_n\}_{n\ge1}$ and positive integers
$$
M_n=\lfloor 2^{nR}\rfloor
$$
such that
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}F\!\left(
\Lambda_n\!\left(\lvert \psi\rangle\!\langle \psi\rvert_{AB}^{\otimes n}\right),
\lvert \Phi_{M_n}\rangle\!\langle \Phi_{M_n}\rvert
\right)=1.
$$
Here
$$
F(\rho,\sigma)=\left\lVert \sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\right\rVert_1
$$
denotes the quantum fidelity.
