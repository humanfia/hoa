Let $\cH=\C^3$ and define
$$
\Phi(X)=\frac{1}{2}\bigl(\Tr(X)\Id-X^T\bigr),
\qquad
X\in\cL(\C^3).
$$
Let $F$ denote the swap operator on $\C^3\otimes\C^3$:
$$
F(\ket{x}\otimes\ket{y})=\ket{y}\otimes\ket{x}.
$$
Compute the Choi matrix of $\Phi$ and prove that
$$
J_\Phi=\frac{1}{2}(\Id\otimes\Id-F)=\Pi_{\ASym^2(\C^3)}.
$$
Conclude that $\Phi$ is completely positive.
