Let $\cH$ be an $n$-qubit Hilbert space, so that
$$
\dim(\cH)=2^n.
$$
Alice encodes a classical random variable $X$ into a quantum ensemble
$$
\{p_x,\rho_x\},
\qquad
\rho_x\in \cS(\cH).
$$
Bob performs a POVM measurement and obtains a classical random variable $Y$.

Using the Holevo bound
$$
I(X:Y)
\leq
S(\rho)-\sum_x p_x S(\rho_x),
\qquad
\rho=\sum_x p_x\rho_x,
$$
prove that
$$
I(X:Y)\leq n.
$$
Thus, $n$ qubits cannot transmit more than $n$ classical bits of information without additional resources.
