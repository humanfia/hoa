Let $\rho,\sigma\in\cS(\cH_A)$, and let
$$
\cN:\cL(\cH_A)\to\cL(\cH_B)
$$
be a completely positive trace-preserving map. Define the trace distance by
$$
D(\omega,\tau)=\frac{1}{2}\|\omega-\tau\|_1.
$$
Prove the data-processing inequality
$$
D(\cN(\rho),\cN(\sigma))
\leq
D(\rho,\sigma).
$$
Equivalently, prove that every quantum channel is a contraction for trace distance on states.
