\Section{Exact Entanglement Dilution to a Maximally Entangled State}
Let $\ket{\eta}_{AB}$ be a bipartite pure state with Schmidt decomposition
$$
\ket{\eta}_{AB}
=
\sum_{i=1}^d
\sqrt{\mu_i}
\ket{i}_A\ket{i}_B,
$$
where
$$
\mu_1\geq \mu_2\geq \cdots \geq \mu_d>0,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^d \mu_i=1.
$$
Let
$$
\ket{\Phi_M}_{AB}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{M}}
\sum_{j=1}^M
\ket{j}_A\ket{j}_B
$$
be the standard maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank $M$.

Using Nielsen's majorization criterion, prove that $\ket{\eta}_{AB}$ can be transformed deterministically into $\ket{\Phi_M}_{AB}$ by LOCC if and only if
$$
\mu_1\leq \frac1M.
$$
