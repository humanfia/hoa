\Section{Converse for Entanglement Concentration}
Let $\lvert \psi\rangle_{AB}\in\mathcal{H}_A\otimes\mathcal{H}_B$ be a bipartite pure state, and let
$$
E(\psi)=S(\psi_A),
\qquad
\psi_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B(\lvert \psi\rangle\!\langle \psi\rvert_{AB}).
$$
For each $n$, let $\lvert \Phi_{M_n}\rangle$ denote the standard maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank
$M_n$:
$$
\lvert \Phi_{M_n}\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{M_n}}\sum_{j=1}^{M_n}\lvert j\rangle_A\lvert j\rangle_B.
$$
Suppose there exist a sequence of positive integers $\{M_n\}_{n\ge1}$ and a sequence of LOCC
channels $\{\Lambda_n\}_{n\ge1}$ such that
$$
\rho_n=\Lambda_n\!\left(\lvert \psi\rangle\!\langle \psi\rvert_{AB}^{\otimes n}\right)
$$
satisfies
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}F(\rho_n,\lvert \Phi_{M_n}\rangle\!\langle \Phi_{M_n}\rvert)=1.
$$
Prove the converse bound
$$
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log_2M_n\le E(\psi).
$$
