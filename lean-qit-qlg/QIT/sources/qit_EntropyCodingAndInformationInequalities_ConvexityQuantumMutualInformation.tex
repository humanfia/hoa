Let
$$
\rho_{AB}
=
\sum_i p_i \rho_{AB}^i
$$
be a convex combination of bipartite density operators on
$$
\cH_A\otimes \cH_B,
$$
where $p_i\geq 0$ and $\sum_i p_i=1$. The quantum conditional entropy is defined as
$$
S(A|B)_\rho
=
S(\rho_{AB})-S(\rho_B),
$$
where
$$
\rho_B=\Tr_A(\rho_{AB}).
$$
The quantum mutual information is defined as
$$
I(A:B)_\rho
=
S(\rho_A)+S(\rho_B)-S(\rho_{AB})
=
S(A)_\rho-S(A|B)_\rho.
$$

State whether $I(A:B)_\rho$ is convex, concave, or neither as a function of $\rho_{AB}$. Use the concavity of conditional entropy to justify your answer.
