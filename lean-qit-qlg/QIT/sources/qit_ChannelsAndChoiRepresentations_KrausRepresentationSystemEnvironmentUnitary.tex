Let $\cH_S\cong\C^2$ and $\cH_E\cong\C^2$, and define
$$
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Y=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-i\\
i&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Let
$$
U=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}X\otimes\Id_E
+
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}Y\otimes X_E,
$$
which is unitary by Problem~\ref{prob:qit-channel-01}. Assume that the environment is initialized in $\ket{0}_E$. Derive the reduced system channel
$$
\cE(\rho_S)
=
\Tr_E\!\left[
U(\rho_S\otimes\ket{0}\bra{0}_E)U^\dagger
\right].
$$
Express $\cE$ in Kraus form,
$$
\cE(\rho_S)=\sum_k E_k\rho_S E_k^\dagger,
$$
identify the Kraus operators $E_k=\bra{k}_E U\ket{0}_E$, and verify $\sum_k E_k^\dagger E_k=\Id_S$.
