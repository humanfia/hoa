Let
$$
\ket{AB}\in \cH_A\otimes \cH_B
$$
be a pure state of a bipartite quantum system shared by Alice and Bob. Define
$$
\rho_{AB}=\ket{AB}\bra{AB},
\qquad
\rho_A=\Tr_B(\rho_{AB}).
$$
The quantum conditional entropy of $B$ given $A$ is
$$
S(B|A)
=
S(\rho_{AB})-S(\rho_A),
$$
where
$$
S(\omega)=-\Tr(\omega\log_2 \omega).
$$

Prove that $\ket{AB}$ is entangled if and only if
$$
S(B|A)<0.
$$
