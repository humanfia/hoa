Let
$$
\mathcal{E}:\cL(\cH)\to \cL(\cH)
$$
be a trace-preserving quantum operation with Kraus representation
$$
\mathcal{E}(\rho)=\sum_i E_i\rho E_i^\dagger.
$$
The entanglement fidelity of $\mathcal{E}$ with respect to an input state $\rho$ is given by
$$
F_e(\rho,\mathcal{E})
=
\sum_i
\left|
\Tr(\rho E_i)
\right|^2.
$$
Equivalently, there exists a preferred Kraus representation for which
$$
F_e(\rho,\mathcal{E})
=
\left|
\Tr(\rho E_1)
\right|^2.
$$

Suppose now that the input state is pure,
$$
\rho=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}.
$$
Let $U$ be a target unitary gate, and let the noisy implementation of $U$ be described by the effective channel
$$
\widetilde{\mathcal{E}}(\rho)
=
U^\dagger \mathcal{E}(\rho) U.
$$
Prove that the entanglement fidelity reduces to the standard pure-state gate fidelity:
$$
F_e(\rho,\widetilde{\mathcal{E}})
=
\bra{\psi}
U^\dagger
\mathcal{E}(\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi})
U
\ket{\psi}.
$$
