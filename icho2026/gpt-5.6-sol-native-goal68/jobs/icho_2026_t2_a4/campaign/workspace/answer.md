# IChO 2026 T2-A4

**Tick the upper-right box.** The phase portrait is traversed **clockwise**.

Starting on the lower horizontal branch, where
\([\mathrm{HBrO_2}]=[\mathrm{HBrO_2}]_B\), the problem states that
\([\mathrm{Br^-}]\) slowly decreases from
\([\mathrm{Br^-}]_{\max}\) to
\([\mathrm{Br^-}]_{\mathrm{critical}}\). Because bromide concentration is the
horizontal coordinate, motion on that branch is from right to left.

The concentrations oscillate around the closed polygon, so the arrows must
form a consistently directed cycle. Continuing from the right-to-left lower
branch gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{lower right}\longrightarrow\text{lower left}
\longrightarrow\text{upper left}\\
&\qquad\longrightarrow\text{upper right}
\longrightarrow\text{lower right},
\end{aligned}
\]

which is clockwise. On the blank student answer sheet this is the diagram in
the upper-right position: its upper arrow points right, its right sloping arrow
points down and right, its lower arrow points left, and its left sloping arrow
points up and left.
