## Scope and method

- Assessed files: `solutions/Q1.md` through `solutions/Q9.md`.
- Preserved source snapshot: Kimi result commit
  `f6360bafbaf7f9d95034d8d75171c3415e013f23`.
- Grading authority: the nine official English IChO 2026 theoretical solution
  PDFs.
- Every numbered subproblem was checked against the printed marking scheme.
  Structure-heavy answers were also compared directly with the official PDF
  drawings rather than relying on extracted text.
- The GPT-5.6 score table was not used to assign Kimi's points.
- A textual structure receives drawing credit only when it uniquely specifies
  the required connectivity, bond orders, substituent positions, charge or
  radical state, and stereochemistry.

This is an independent rubric reconstruction, not a score issued by the IChO
jury. Chemical-drawing equivalence remains judgment-sensitive.
