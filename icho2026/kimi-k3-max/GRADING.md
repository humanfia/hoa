## Convention and status

A textual chemical structure receives drawing credit only when it uniquely
specifies the official connectivity, substituents, and required
stereochemistry. The official English answer PDFs are the grading authority.
This is a strict rubric-based reconstruction, not a score issued by the IChO
jury. Some drawing scores necessarily involve evaluator judgment.

The complete first-round-versus-final grading ledger, including subpart-level
changes and reproducibility arithmetic, is preserved in
