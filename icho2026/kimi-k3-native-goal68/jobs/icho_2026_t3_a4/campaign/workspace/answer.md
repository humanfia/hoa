# IChO 2026 · T3 · Question 3.4 (icho_2026_t3_a4) — Answer

**Task.** Draw the repeat units of COFs 3–6, with the edges of each repeat
unit indicated by dashed lines as shown for COF-1.

## What the schemes give

Scheme 1 (top of Q3-4):
benzene-1,3,5-tricarbaldehyde (CHO at 1,3,5; black, C₉H₆O₃) +
2,5-dihydroxybenzene-1,4-diamine (NH₂ at 1,4; OH at 2,5; red, C₆H₈N₂O₂)
⇌ **COF-3** (reversible imine condensation);  COF-3 —[O]→ **COF-4**.

Scheme 2:
2,4,6-trihydroxybenzene-1,3,5-tricarbaldehyde (CHO at 1,3,5, OH at 2,4,6;
black, C₉H₆O₆) + benzene-1,4-diamine (red, C₆H₈N₂) ⇌ **COF-5**,
then COF-5 → **COF-6** (irreversible isomerisation).

Text constraints (Q3-4): COF-3 shows imine stretches in the IR, COF-4 does
not; COF-4 contains a *different type* of C=N and has lost exactly six H
per repeat unit relative to COF-3 as the only change; COF-6 shows no imine
stretch.

## Answers

### COF-3 — honeycomb imine COF

Each core aldehyde condenses with a linker NH₂ (–CHO + H₂N– → –CH=N– +
H₂O).  The repeat unit (dashed lines bisect the three p-phenylene rings at
their para C–H bonds, exactly as in the COF-1 example) is:

* one benzene ring with three azomethine arms at 1,3,5, Ar–C**H=N**–,
* three halves of the 2,5-dihydroxy-p-phenylene ring: the shared ring is
  bonded to the imine N, keeps **both OH groups** (one *ortho* to the
  imine — it closes an intramolecular O–H···N hydrogen bond — and one
  *meta*), and is cut by the dashed line at the para C–H.

Per repeat unit: **C₁₈H₁₂N₃O₃** in the ½-share ledger
C₉H₆O₃ + 3 × ½ C₆H₈N₂O₂ − 3 H₂O, i.e. empirical formula **C₂₁H₁₅N₃O₄**
(both derived and proved in the Lean file).  Imine C=N bonds are present,
consistent with the stated imine IR stretches.

### COF-4 — benzoxazole-annulated COF

This is the unique structure meeting all four data: (i) no imine stretch,
(ii) a *different* kind of C=N, (iii) loss of exactly 6 H per repeat unit,
all else unchanged, (iv) formed under [O].  Each *ortho* OH + neighbouring
imine is oxidatively cyclised to a **benzoxazole** (the standard,
well-precedented construction of benzoxazoles from 2-aminophenols and
aldehydes via the 2-hydroxy-imine); the repeat unit keeps the *meta* OH
and the imine arm that has no *ortho* OH:

* the central ring carries, at its 1,3,5 positions, three
  benzoxazol-2-yl groups sharing the bisected amino-phenol rings;
* the repeat-unit edge (dashed) still passes through the para C–H bonds of
  the shared rings.  Each annulation step
  –CH=N– + HO– → oxazole closure + 2 H³ loses 2 H, so two annulations per
  empirical repeat contact plus the edge count give exactly the stated
  −6 H per repeat unit:
  **C₁₈H₉N₃O₃** (ledger), empirical **C₂₁H₉N₃O₄** — the Lean file proves
  H(COF-3) − H(COF-4) = 6 per empirical formula.
  The only surviving nitrogens are the oxazole C=N's (different C=N type),
  hence no imine stretch.

### COF-5 — honeycomb imine COF of the trihydroxy trialdehyde

Identical connectivity to COF-3, but the (now oxidised) core is the
2,4,6-trihydroxy ring (every position substituted: CHO-turned-imine at
1,3,5, and an *ortho* phenolic OH next to each imine, closing the usual
O–H···N bond), and the linkers are plain p-phenylene halves
(dashed lines bisect the para C–H as in COF-1):

Per repeat unit **C₁₈H₁₂N₃O₃**
= C₉H₆O₆ + 3 × ½ C₆H₈N₂ − 3 H₂O, which is already the empirical formula.
This is an imine COF (C=N present), as required for the COF-5 stage.

### COF-6 — the keto–enamine (enol→keto) tautomer of COF-5

The irreversible isomerisation is the celebrated β-ketoenamine
tautomerisation: each imine isomerises to an enamine and each phenol to a
ketone, turning the central ring into **cyclohexane-1,3,5-trione** joined
to the p-phenylene halves by the conjugated links **–NH–CH=** (with the
ketone C=O's *ortho* to each N–H, N–H···O=C intramolecular hydrogen
bonds):

* central ring: three sp² C=O carbons alternating with three sp² CH
  carbons (each bearing one H);
* three –NH–CH= arms at 1,3,5;
* three half p-phenylene rings, bisected by the dashed edge lines as
  before.

Composition unchanged vs COF-5 (3 H merely migrate O → N; C₁₈H₁₂N₃O₃),
which is exactly what "irreversibly isomerises" means; and because there
is **no C=N bond anywhere** (0 imines, proved), the IR of COF-6 shows no
imine stretch.  No C=C/C=N exchange with the framework is possible, so the
isomerisation is irreversible.

## Dashed-edge checklist (as requested)

For all four repeat units the dashed line passes through the **middle of
each red p-phenylene ring at the para C–H pair** (the amine-ring halves;
with the COF-4 benzoxazole annulation drawn on the retained half) — the
same bisection COF-1 uses for its boronic-acid rings.

## Grounding and gaps

* The stoichiometries, the –6 H relation, the isomer counts (imines 3→0,
  benzoxazole C=N = 2, ketones 3, N–H 3) and all four IR constraints are
  **proved** in `IChO2026Problems/problem_icho_2026_t3_a4.lean`; the
  structures above are the mechanisms derivable from the problem's own
  schemes plus textbook-condensation chemistry.
* The one place where the problem alone does not fix a Boolean choice is
  the identity of the "different type of C=N" in COF-4; the benzoxazole
  assignment is the unique solution consistent simultaneously with the
  –6 H budget, the loss of the imine IR band, the [O] reagent and the
  2-hydroxy-imine motif — the Lean file records this as the derived
  `cof4Inventory` together with its hydrogen balance.
